What we consider a lead

Every lead is 100% exclusive to you and never shared with another electrician. Each one includes:

  • Name
  • Email
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Whether they already have a charger
  • Charger type
  • How far the panel is from the charger
  • When they want it installed

Leads come to you by email, text and a shared spreadsheet. Electricians running these campaigns close 30–60% of them.

  • 24 hours after you approve it: ads go live in your service area.
  • Within 72 hours: leads start landing in your inbox.

We move fast on purpose. Every week your ads aren't running is a week of jobs going to someone else.

What happens after the 30-day trial?

The pattern is almost always the same: we start you small on leads, make you some money, and then move up from there.

A good example is a New Jersey contractor Ethan worked with at Ad Blitz. They started at $1–2K a month in ad spend. Today they spend $20–30K a month and do $500–600K months, mostly from generators. They didn't get there on day one. It started with a low-cost 30-day trial.

So don't think of the trial as the whole relationship. Think of it as the handshake. Once leads are flowing and you're closing, the bigger gains usually come from the next layers: your offer, your comp plan, your closer, your follow-up system, even other channels. We help with all of that.

We don't take everyone on, either. We turn down people who apply, based on how the trial goes and how you treat our team.
