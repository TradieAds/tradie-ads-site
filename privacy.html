Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 13541398 Canada Inc., operating as Tradie Ads ("Tradie Ads", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit tradie-ads.com, contact us, chat with us or receive text messages from us.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, business name, email address, phone number, service area and any details you share when you chat with us, call, email or book a call.
  • Information collected automatically: basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and the date and time of your visit, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the services you ask for.
  • To schedule and confirm calls and appointments.
  • To send you text messages and emails you have agreed to receive, including account notifications, appointment reminders, customer care and, where you have opted in, marketing messages.
  • To operate, maintain and improve our website and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. SMS / text messaging

If you opt in to receive text messages from us, we use your mobile number only to send the messages you agreed to. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out and HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from all the information-sharing categories described in this policy and will not be shared with or sold to any third party.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers who help us run our business (for example hosting, scheduling, email and messaging platforms), who may use it only to provide services to us;
  • when required by law, or to protect our rights, safety or property;
  • as part of a merger, sale or transfer of our business, subject to this policy.

The SMS restrictions in section 3 apply in every case above.

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, understand how it is used and improve it. You can turn off cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work properly.

6. Data retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law. We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link and of texts by replying STOP. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws, such as Canada's PIPEDA, British Columbia's PIPA or US state laws like California's. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
