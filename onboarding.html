The road map

  • 1Days 1–20 · Kickoff. Tracking set up, first ads live, and several angles tested at once to find what makes your customers stop scrolling.
  • 2Days 20–40 · Testing. The quiet part. Results can swing day to day: zero leads one day, five to ten the next. That's normal, not a sign anything's broken.
  • 3Days 40–60 · Stability. Winning ads pull ahead, cost per lead comes down and stays down, and we start talking about scaling.
  • 4Days 60–90 · Profit. Real revenue traced back to the campaigns.
  • 5Day 90+ · Autopilot. A lead engine you don't have to think about.

Every business is different and some move faster. Think of this as a map.
